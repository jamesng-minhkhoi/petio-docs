# Pet profiles

Every animal in your household gets a profile that family members can share.

## Profile contents

- Basics: name, species, breed, birthday, weight
- Vital stats and weight history (see [Health records](health.md))
- Linked documents: vaccinations, certificates, prescriptions
  (see [Documents](documents.md))
