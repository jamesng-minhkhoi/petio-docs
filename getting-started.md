# Getting started

Get Petio running in a few minutes: install, sign in, and set up your first pet.

## Install

- **iOS:** [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/petio-pet-care-companion/id6745301343)
- **Android:** coming soon

## Sign in

Petio supports sign-in with Apple and Google, plus email. Your account is
where household sharing, backups, and Peti's assistant session history live.

## Create your first pet

1. On sign-in you are walked through completing a profile for your pet.
2. Add the basics: name, species, breed, birthday.
3. Invite family members to share the pet profile (see
   [Family sharing](user-guide/families.md)).
